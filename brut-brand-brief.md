# BRUT. — Demo Brand Brief

> This fictional brand is used as the running example across lessons 03–08 of the Vibe Coding for Marketers course.

---

## Company

**BRUT.**
Tagline: *Nothing added. Nothing to hide.*
Founded 2023, Ghent, Belgium. Modern craft cider for a new generation.

---

## Brand Story

Started by three friends (Roos, Sander, and Wout) in a garage in Ghent. Background in design and hospitality, zero experience in brewing. They wanted a drink that fits their life — clean, no sugar, no bullshit. Belgium has great beer culture, but they saw a gap: no one was making cider that looked and tasted like it belongs in 2026.

They source apples from small Flemish orchards, ferment everything dry, and keep the ingredient list embarrassingly short. The branding is as minimal as the product. First year: local bars and festivals. Now: Benelux-wide, launching into Germany and France.

## Visual Style

### Design Philosophy

BRUT. looks the way it tastes — stripped back, nothing unnecessary. The visual identity borrows from contemporary packaging design and editorial fashion, not from the craft beverage category. We don't look like a cider brand. That's intentional.

### Typography

- **Primary typeface:** General Sans (or Satoshi) — geometric grotesk, clean but not cold
- **Headlines:** Bold/Semibold, oversized (48–120px on web), tight tracking (-0.02em), uppercase for product names only
- **Body:** Regular weight, 16–18px, generous line-height (1.6). Readable, unhurried.
- **Hierarchy through size and weight only** — no decorative fonts, no serifs, no scripts. Ever.

### Layout Principles

- **Aggressive whitespace** — let elements breathe. Minimum 80px section padding on desktop.
- **Grid:** Simple 12-column, content rarely wider than 8 columns. Off-center compositions welcome.
- **Asymmetry over symmetry** — left-aligned text blocks, off-grid image placement, intentional tension.
- **No borders, no drop shadows, no rounded corners.** Clean edges. Flat surfaces.
- **Cards and containers:** Subtle background color shifts (warm white → soft grey) instead of outlines.

### Photography Direction

- **Product shots:** Straight-on, single can, centered on solid color background matching the product accent color. Hard studio lighting. No props, no garnishes, no "lifestyle clutter."
- **Lifestyle shots:** Urban, European, candid. Golden hour bar terraces. Friends sharing without posing. Shot on film or graded to feel analog — slight grain, muted highlights, warm shadows.
- **Never:** Stock photography. Overhead flat-lays. Apples on a wooden table. Anything that screams "craft beverage brand."

### Iconography & Graphics

- **No icons** unless absolutely functional (nav menu, cart, social links).
- **No illustrations.** No hand-drawn elements. No patterns.
- **Dividers:** Thin 1px lines in soft grey (`#B0ADA8`), used sparingly.
- **Hover/interaction states:** Electric lime (`#C8FF00`) — used only for interactive elements. Never decorative.

### Motion & Interaction (Web)

- **Subtle, intentional.** No bouncing, no parallax, no scroll-jacking.
- **Fade-in on scroll** (opacity 0 → 1, translateY 20px, 0.4s ease) for text blocks and images.
- **Hover on CTAs:** Background fill from transparent to electric lime, text inverts to off-black. Instant. No slow transitions.
- **Page transitions:** Simple crossfade, 0.2s max.

### Do / Don't

| ✓ Do | ✗ Don't |
|---|---|
| Large type, few words | Walls of small text |
| One hero image per section | Image collages or carousels |
| Let whitespace do the work | Fill every pixel |
| Color accents only for function | Decorative color blocks |
| Flat, clean, editorial | Glossy, 3D, beverage-industry cliché |
| Film-grain lifestyle photos | Polished stock photography |

## Target Audience

21–32. Urban. Design-aware. Drinks natural wine, matcha, craft stuff. Doesn't want a "beer alternative" — wants something that fits their aesthetic and values. Instagram-native.

---

## Brand Voice

Dry, confident, short sentences. No exclamation marks. No emojis. Not trying to please everyone — and that's fine.

### Examples

- "We make cider. Dry cider. That's it."
- "Four ingredients. We could list them, but you'd get bored."
- "No added sugar. Not because it's trendy. Because sugar doesn't belong here."
- "Our apples come from Flemish orchards. Small ones. The kind Google Maps doesn't know about."
- "You'll find us in bars that care. Not in vending machines."
- "We don't do seasonal flavors. We do four things well."
- "BRUT. Cassis — dark fruit, dry finish, no explanation needed."
- "Brewed in Ghent. Not brewed for everyone."

### Rules

- Max 8–10 words per sentence where possible
- Periods over commas. Fragments are fine.
- Lowercase energy even when capitalized
- Never explain the joke
- Never use: "amazing", "unique", "passionate", "crafted with love"
